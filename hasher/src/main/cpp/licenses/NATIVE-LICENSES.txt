The code of other people in the hasher's library
================================================

PegasusBridge hashes a game's file with a library of its own, librahasher
(rahasher.dll on Windows, librahasher.so in the Android app). PegasusBridge
itself is under the GNU General Public License, version 3, whose text comes
with it as LICENSE. The library is compiled from the sources of the projects
below as well, each under a licence of its own, and those licences ask that
their text travel with every copy of what was compiled from them. Here they
are, each as its authors wrote it.

What of each project is compiled, and from which copy, is written down in
the source tree beside the copies: hasher/src/main/cpp/rcheevos.version,
libchdr.version and rahasher.sources.
